# Option H: Moonlit Marble

The heirloom set. A pale veined marble slab floating in the Nightfall dusk, with
the Night Sky stones — deep jewel tones under a wet polish — sitting dark in
pale wells. The inverse of every other direction here, where light stones sit on
a dark board.

Import these layers into Icon Composer in numeric order:
1. `01-dusk.svg`
2. `02-marble.svg`
3. `03-wells.svg`
4. `04-stones.svg`
5. `05-light.svg`

Recommended Icon Composer settings:
- Liquid Glass on the stones group, Individual mode, specular **high**. The
  Night Sky finish is roughness 0.04 / clearcoat 1.0 — nearly a mirror — and the
  small hard speculars are the only thing separating these stones from the dark.
- Marble stays opaque. It is stone, not glass, and Liquid Glass on the slab
  turns the veins to mush.
- The dusk gradient is the app's `nightfall` backdrop with its travelling glow
  parked top-right. If it moves, move the slab's chamfer highlight to match.
